Our School Story

Our Story

Divine Grace Schools was established on September 4, 2000, by Dr. and Mrs. Sam O. Aluko in Ifofin, Ilesa, Osun State, with a commitment to providing quality education that promotes academic excellence, good character, discipline, and sound moral values. In 2008, the school obtained accreditation to present students for external examinations, marking a significant milestone in its academic journey.

Over the years, the school has continued to grow, expanding with the establishment of the Zion Campus in Aralopon, Irojo, Ilesa. Today, Divine Grace Schools provides education across Pre-Primary, Primary, and Secondary levels, nurturing learners through academic development, godly values, and character formation, while preparing them to make meaningful contributions to society.

Founder's Message

A Word from Our Founder

Welcome to Divine Grace Schools!

Since our establishment on September 4, 2000, we have remained committed to providing quality education that combines academic excellence with strong moral values, discipline, and character development.

At Divine Grace Schools, we believe that education goes beyond the classroom. It is about discovering potential, nurturing talents, building confidence, and preparing young people to become responsible and productive members of society.

We continually strive to provide a supportive learning environment, embrace innovation and modern teaching methods, and equip our students with the knowledge, skills, and values needed to thrive in an ever-changing world.

We recognise that educating a child is a shared responsibility, and we deeply value the trust and partnership of our parents, dedicated staff, and wider community.

As we look to the future, our commitment remains unwavering: to raise well-rounded individuals who are academically sound, morally grounded, and prepared to make meaningful contributions to society.

Thank you for being part of our journey.

Dr. Sam O. Aluko Director, Divine Grace Schools

What Guides Us

Our Vision and Mission

Our Vision

To become a leading educational institution at the local, state, and national levels, recognised for academic excellence, discipline, character formation, godliness and educational innovation.

Our Mission

To provide a supportive and disciplined learning environment in which students develop academically, morally, spiritually, socially and practically, while preparing them to succeed in an increasingly competitive world.

What We Stand For

Our Core Values

Godliness

Living by godly principles and maintaining a strong moral foundation.

Academic Excellence

Pursuing knowledge, developing abilities and striving for outstanding academic performance.

Honesty

Speaking truthfully, acting with integrity and taking responsibility for our actions.

Obedience

Discipline

Respect

Student Policy

Our Expectations of Students

At Divine Grace Schools, we expect every student to demonstrate discipline, honesty, respect, and a genuine commitment to learning. Our students are encouraged to take responsibility for their actions, uphold good morals, respect others, and develop the character and confidence needed to make positive contributions to society.

  • Academic Commitment and IntegrityStudents are expected to participate actively in lessons, complete assignments, strive for continuous improvement, and uphold honesty in all academic activities, including tests and examinations.
  • Discipline and RespectStudents must obey legitimate school instructions, follow established rules, treat others with courtesy, maintain good conduct, and take responsibility for their behaviour and the school environment.

Teachers' Policy

Our Standards for Teachers

At Divine Grace Schools, we expect our teachers to demonstrate professionalism, dedication, integrity, and a commitment to effective teaching. Every teacher is responsible for creating a supportive learning environment, guiding students' academic development, upholding the school's values, and contributing positively to the wider school community.

  • Effective Teaching and AssessmentTeachers are expected to prepare and deliver engaging lessons, follow the approved curriculum, assess students regularly, maintain accurate academic records, and provide appropriate feedback to support learning.
  • Professional Conduct and DevelopmentTeachers must demonstrate punctuality, integrity, responsible classroom management, and respect for students and colleagues. They are also expected to participate in professional development and carry out their assigned responsibilities.
