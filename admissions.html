How to Apply

Our Admission Process in Four Steps

  1. STEP 1

    Apply for Admission

    Parents obtain and complete the school's admission application form, either online or using a downloadable copy.

  2. STEP 2

    Submit Required Documents

    Provide the previous school result, the candidate's birth certificate, proof of application fee payment and a recent passport photograph.

  3. STEP 3

    Entrance Examination and Admission Offer

    Candidates follow the school's entrance examination process, and successful candidates receive an admission offer.

  4. STEP 4

    Accept the Admission Offer

    Parents complete the acceptance documentation and follow the school's instructions for required payments.
