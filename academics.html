Academic Overview

Nurturing Excellence Through Learning

At Divine Grace Schools, we believe education goes beyond the acquisition of knowledge. We are committed to providing an environment where learners develop academically, socially and morally. Our approach encourages curiosity, critical thinking, discipline and a love for learning, while promoting godly values and strong character. We aim to equip every learner with the knowledge, skills and confidence to recognise their potential, pursue excellence and make meaningful contributions to society.

Academic Levels

Our Academic Levels

Our academic programme is organised into three levels: Creche, Primary and Secondary. Each level provides an important stage in a learner's educational journey, supporting their growth and preparing them for the next stage of learning.

Early Years

Creche & Nursery

A caring, structured start where our youngest learners build confidence, curiosity and good habits.

Primary

Primary School

A strong foundation in core skills, character and godly values, in a supportive and disciplined environment.

Junior Secondary

Junior Secondary School (JSS)

Students deepen their knowledge, think independently and grow in discipline and responsibility.

Senior Secondary

Senior Secondary School (SSS)

Focused academic preparation for external examinations and for the next chapter of life.

Practical Learning

Science Laboratory

Practical work in Physics and Chemistry that brings classroom learning to life.

Practical Learning

ICT Laboratory

Practical computer studies that build digital skills for today’s learners.

Competitions & Enrichment

Beyond the Classroom

At Divine Grace Schools, learning extends beyond the classroom through opportunities that challenge learners to think critically, explore their abilities and demonstrate their knowledge. We encourage participation in academic competitions, intellectual activities and educational engagements that build confidence, promote healthy competition and inspire a spirit of excellence. These experiences help our learners develop problem-solving skills, broaden their perspectives and discover the value of continuous learning.

  • External academic competitions
  • Quizzes
  • Olympiads
  • Debates and other intellectual activities
